Emblems shown under the tablet's page list in the Elite, Babylon 5 and Star Wars themes (static/themes/*.css).

them. These files are not under the project's GPL: each keeps the terms given here.

Elite Dangerous
  elite.webp: the Explorer "Elite" rank badge (the Pilots' Federation's wings and phoenix), "Explorer Elite icon.png"
    on the Elite Dangerous Wiki (elite-dangerous.fandom.com), recoloured from its teals to two dark oranges
    (brightness mapped from #6A3000 to #A04C00, transparency kept) and saved as WebP. Used under Frontier's
    Elite Dangerous media usage rules
    (https://customersupport.frontier.co.uk/hc/en-us/articles/4404292442642-How-can-I-use-Elite-Dangerous-media):
    Assets borrowed from Elite Dangerous, with permission of Frontier Developments plc.

Babylon 5 (babylon5.webp: the Earth Alliance seal, narn.webp: the Narn Regime, minbari.webp: the Minbari Federation,
centauri.webp: the Centauri Republic, vorlon.webp: the Vorlon Empire)
  User-created redrawings released into the public domain by their authors on the Babylon 5 Wiki
  (babylon5.fandom.com; Category:User-created public domain images):
    EAS_wiki.png, Wiki_narnregime.png, Wiki_minbarifed.png, Wiki_centaurirep.png, Wiki_vorlonemp.png (by Blind Wolf,
    "Emblem of the Vorlon Empire", licensing {{Self}}; the wiki's CDN serves it as WebP, kept unchanged)
  shadow.webp: an emblem for the Shadows, an AI-generated image the author made (2026-10-12), loosely based on the
    design of the Shadows' ships; recoloured from black to the theme's purples (brightness mapped from #5A2D8C to
    #E4DCF0, transparency kept), scaled to 512 x 512 and saved as WebP.
  The emblems themselves are Babylon 5's (c) Warner Bros.
